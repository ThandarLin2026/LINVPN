SSH (Secure Shell) SSH tunneling is one of the most widely used methods for secure internet access. It creates an encrypted tunnel between your device and the server. Advantages: - High security - Works on most networks - Simple to use Disadvantages: - Slightly slower than modern protocols --- 2. V2Ray (VMess / VLESS) V2Ray is a powerful and flexible protocol designed to bypass censorship and deep packet inspection (DPI). Advantages: - Extremely fast and stable - Hard to detect by ISPs - Supports multiple transport methods Disadvantages: - Slightly complex setup --- 3. UDP (User Datagram Protocol) UDP-based VPNs are optimized for speed and performance, especially for gaming and streaming. Advantages: - Very fast - Low latency - Ideal for real-time applications Disadvantages: - Less reliable than TCP --- 4. OpenVPN (OVPN) OpenVPN is one of the most trusted VPN protocols, widely used worldwide. Advantages: - Strong encryption - Highly configurable - Open-source and secure Disadvantages: - Requires additional app setup --- 5. Shadowsocks Shadowsocks is a lightweight proxy protocol designed to bypass internet restrictions. Advantages: - Fast and lightweight - Effective against censorship - Easy to deploy Disadvantages: - Not a full VPN (proxy-based) --- 6. Trojan Trojan protocol disguises VPN traffic as normal HTTPS traffic, making it nearly impossible to detect.
